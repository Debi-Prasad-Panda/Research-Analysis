Layer 1: Input Representation

Token Embedding

Section 3.4

Converts discrete tokens into continuous vector representations.

Positional Encoding

Section 3.5

Injects relative/absolute position info since the model has no recurrence.

$$ PE_{(pos, 2i)} = \sin(pos/10000^{2i/d_{model}}) $$

Layer 2: Encoder Block

visibility Multi-Head Attention

Section 3.2.2

Allows the model to jointly attend to information from different representation subspaces.

$$ \text{Attn}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V $$

Position-Wise FFN

Section 3.3

Applied to each position separately and identically.

$$ \text{FFN}(x) = \max(0, xW_1 + b_1)W_2 + b_2 $$
